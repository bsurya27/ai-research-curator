A multi-agent system for personal research

My morning feed, curated by agents that learn from what I read.

Good ideas need good input. I wasn't getting enough of it. So, I built this small multi-agent system to break a familiar creative block.

A curator agent wakes up every morning on an EC2 instance and reads the signals I left from the previous day. It goes out and fetches the latest papers, discussions, and opinions, scores everything against my taste, and writes me a personalized briefing. Then shuts itself down. A reporter agent lives in an always-on interface where I read, rate, and chat. It quietly captures how I interact and feeds it back, so by the next morning the curator knows me a little better than it did the day before. If I'm reading the right things every day, the ideas will come.
